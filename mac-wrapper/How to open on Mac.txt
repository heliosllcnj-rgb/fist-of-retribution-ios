Fist of Retribution: Project Zero - Mac (Apple silicon: M1, M2, M3, M4 and later)

1. Unzip FistOfRetribution-Mac.zip and drag "Fist of Retribution.app" into Applications (or leave it anywhere).
2. The app isn't from the App Store, so the first time macOS blocks it:
   - Double-click it once. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Fist of Retribution,
     then confirm with "Open Anyway" and your password.
   If macOS instead says the app "is damaged", open Terminal and run:
     xattr -cr "/Applications/Fist of Retribution.app"
   then open it again.
3. After that it opens normally. Keyboard and controller settings are in Options; saves live in
   ~/Library/Application Support/FistOfRetribution.
